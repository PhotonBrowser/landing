# Photon Browser — logo guide

Use the SVG master whenever possible. Keep the original proportions and leave clear space of at least 10% of the logo height. Use the color logo on light surfaces, and the white version on dark surfaces. Monochrome versions intentionally merge color divisions.

Palette: #76b4fb, #bbd9fc, #5e5dfb. Colors are estimated from the uploaded raster, not independently verified brand specifications.

Inspect 16px and 32px icons at their actual size. These exports preserve the original geometry; tiny icons have not been optically redrawn. No brand typeface has been selected; CSS uses a system fallback.

Mockups are digital presentation images on reusable photographic templates. They are not production, embroidery or print files. Technical assets are rendered directly from the stored SVG.
